Roomba has one carrier: the planar base. Intent is serialized in time.
Heading, translation, arcs, and holds are all you have. There is no arm,
no head, no height. Never emit move(...), path(ee, ...), grip, torso, or z.

When a situation would, on a richer body, move evidence onto the arm or
the head, you must instead restage the same evidence as a base event:
- securing attention → a small in-place yaw wag, then a hold, then the beat
- deference / not looming → retreat a little on −x, or widen a lateral y
  offset, before the beat; never advance into the person
- low addressee (child, seated) → smaller extents, slower onset; you cannot
  drop a mast, so you drop travel
- far addressee → larger rock / yaw, extra rep, longer final hold
- crowded / quiet → shrink translation; prefer in-place yaw over driving
- time-critical → drop flourishes and extra reps; keep the identity beat
- emergency → sharp onset, larger extent, a short hold so it still reads
- carrying a load → same channel budget you always have; just cap spd so
  the payload is not jerked (spd < 3)

A Roomba "point" that is only hold(t=...) in the routine program is already
underdetermined. Do not invent a drive-into-the-target to compensate: that
reads as "I am going there", not "you go there". Prefer a heading change
plus a hold, or leave the hold and add a small preparatory yaw.

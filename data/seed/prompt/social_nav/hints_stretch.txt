Stretch has three independent carriers that can run together:
  1. base path  — where the robot is going
  2. arm + mast — where you should go, or what is being indicated
     (dir, grip, x, z, torso, elbow; path(ee, ...); move(shoulder|elbow|wrist|torso))
  3. head       — who is being addressed, what is being attended to
     (head=; move(head, pan|tilt, ...))

Use the extra channels. Do not serialize everything onto the base the way a
Roomba must. Prefer:
- securing attention on the head, leaving the arm free to deliver the beat
- deference as torso=low and a small −x, not as a long retreat
- a seated or child addressee as z / torso dropping toward their eye height
- a far or crowded addressee as z / torso rising so the stroke clears bodies
- time-critical as cutting base travel first, keeping the arm beat
- emergency as a crisp arm/head onset, not a spin
- carrying a load: the arm is gone. Re-express the identity beat on head
  and/or base. pose(...) may keep only head= (and dir=still). No path(ee),
  no move(shoulder|elbow|wrist|torso), no grip/torso/elbow/x/y/z.

`&` means two steps run together (e.g. a head turn during a base arc). Use
it when the situation needs simultaneous evidence. Join sequential steps
with `+`.

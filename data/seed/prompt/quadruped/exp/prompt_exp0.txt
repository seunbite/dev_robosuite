[Coordinate frame]
**+x = forward**, **+y = robot left**, **+z = up**.

[Pose]
`pose(stance=stand|sit|crouch|play_bow|threelegs_front)`

Stance → front_height + back_height:
| stance            | front | back | silhouette |
|-------------------|-------|------|------------|
| stand             | mid   | mid  | upright ready |
| sit               | high  | low  | haunches down |
| crouch            | low   | low  | compressed / charge |
| play_bow          | low   | high | front down, rear up |
| threelegs_front   | mid   | mid  | stand + lift right-front paw forward |

Only one three-leg mode: `stance=threelegs_front` (legacy `three_legs=front|up` maps to front).

Optional overrides (advanced): `front_height=low|mid|high`, `back_height=low|mid|high`.

Unsupported / rejected (do **not** emit):
- `pose(head=…)` / `move(head, …)` — no neck; parser drops these (or maps head pan to body).
- `stance=roll_over` / `rear_up` / `front_legs=extend` — not in the renderable bank.
- For “yes/no/curious”, prefer **front_legs z bob**, **base arc yaw**, or **play_bow hold** — never head joints.

[Move]
`move(front_legs|forelegs|back_legs, x|z, degrees, rep=…, spd=…, hold=…)`
- Pair legs: **x or z only**.
- **z sign (planted bob)**: `−` → flex (like a soft play_bow), `+` → extend (like sit front). Feet stay planted; amplitude is small (nod), not a full crouch/bow slam.
- **x**: reach forward/back via foot IK.
- Degrees may be angles (`[-22,22]`) or small meter-like offsets (`-0.15`); parser scales |v|≤1 to degrees when needed.

`move(left_front|right_front|left_back|right_back, x|y|z, …)`
- Single leg: all axes allowed. Prefer with `stance=threelegs_front`.
- Use `right_front` for fist-pump / wave / salute proxies.

[Path]
`path(base, line, x=±m, spd=…, hold=…, rep=…)`
`path(base, arc, r=…, deg=…, spd=…, hold=…, rep=…)`
- `r=0` + `deg=±…` ≈ in-place yaw: `±90` alternates CW then CCW each half (×`rep`).
- `r>0` + `deg=360|720` = forward-walking circles (nose first); `rep` repeats full sweeps.
- Non-stand pose before a line/arc auto-rises to stand, then locomotes.

[Hold]
`hold(t=…)` — freeze current stance / paw for readability (parser → empty movement hold).

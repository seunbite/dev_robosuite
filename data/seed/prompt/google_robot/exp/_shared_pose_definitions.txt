Coordinate frame (world): +x = forward toward the human viewer, +y = robot left, +z = up (ceiling). Right arm is the primary gesturing arm unless left_arm=mirror.

1. arm_position — dominant reach direction of the right arm (front|back|in|out|up|down).
2. gripper_orientation — projected jaw-opening line on plane ⊥ to reach: horizontal (ㅡ) or vertical (|).
3. torso_height — low|mid|high body elevation.
4. head — center|left|right|up|down gaze preset.
5. left_arm — still|mirror or independent arm_position for bilateral poses.
6. x/y/z — optional EE percentile (0–100) refining pose within the same arm_position + gripper_orientation class.
